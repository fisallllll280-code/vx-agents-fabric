# Ω-SYMBOL Office Brief v1

Use this brief when VAIXLNS offices receive work about Unicode emoji, glyphs, icon inventories, or symbol-based interaction.

## Required task envelope

- exact requested outcome and affected product/system;
- target Unicode/Emoji release and source URI;
- local source file hash, if data is available;
- target language among the nine NXL identities recovered from the NEXENT registry;
- target platform, locale, font/runtime, and accessibility requirements;
- permitted read/write scope and separate execution approval, where applicable.

## Office routing

- `Ω.RES`: retrieve and compare normative versions, CLDR labels, and source lineage.
- `Ω.SYMBOL`: parse and index exact sequences; reject unknown or duplicate sequence data.
- `Ω.ENG`: design the IR and language adapters against the recovered NEXENT NXL registry; leave translation on HOLD until formal grammar and semantics exist.
- `Ω.DESIGN`: assess text alternatives, contrast, visual ambiguity, and rendering fallbacks.
- `Ω.PARENT`: independently challenge source, version, safety, compatibility, and proof claims.
- `ARC-X`: issue assurance only from reproducible evidence tied to exact inputs and versions.

## Non-negotiable interpretation rules

A glyph is presentation data. It does not authenticate a user, authorize an action, prove a claim, define a person's identity, or replace a typed value. Labels are locale-sensitive metadata. Unknown sequences, missing source data, or renderer gaps result in HOLD. The recovered NXL registry currently marks all nine languages PROPOSED and supplies placeholder grammar/semantics; translation remains HOLD until formal language specifications and compilers exist.

Do not silently rewrite historical labels. Preserve source entries and add derived metadata with locale, CLDR release, method, and reviewer provenance.
